title: New MS Office to .MD converter
date: 2024-12-14
categories: [tools]
---

**MarkItDown** from Microsoft converts `.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.pptx` and PDFs into Markdown.

Useful for two things: getting documents out of Office and into a repo or a notes vault, and feeding documents to an LLM, which handles Markdown far better than a binary blob.

Google Docs can also export Markdown directly now, if that's where your documents live.

[github.com/microsoft/markitdown](https://github.com/microsoft/markitdown)
