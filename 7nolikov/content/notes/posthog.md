date: 2024-12-31

Open-source product analytics: events, funnels, session replay and feature flags in one place, self-hostable if you want the data on your own servers.

The reason I picked it over the alternatives is not the feature list. It has a permanent free allowance and a **hard $0 billing cap** - not "generous limits", an actual cap you can set. For a side project, that is the difference between analytics and a surprise invoice.

[posthog.com](https://posthog.com)
