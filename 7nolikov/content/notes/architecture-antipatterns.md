A catalogue of architecture antipatterns, each with the symptoms, why people end up there, and what to do instead.

Useful in the specific moment where you can feel a design is wrong but cannot name it yet. Having the name makes the argument possible - it is much easier to say "this is a distributed monolith" than to describe the smell from scratch in a meeting.
